Emotion-aware by design

A good plan is not always enough.

Procrastination is often tied to mood regulation. Locked treats emotional awareness as part of planning, not as an afterthought.

01 · Perceive

Check in quickly

Simple mood and focus prompts help students notice how they feel before planning.

02 · Use

Match work to energy

Harder work can be scheduled for times when a student is better able to focus.

03 · Understand

See the cause

Students can spot why tasks are piling up instead of seeing only the backlog.

04 · Manage

Make work manageable

Break large assignments into smaller pieces, space heavy days, and add breaks near exams.

Go-to-market

Start at the University of Miami. Learn, then expand.

The proposed launch offers the product free during year one, measures student interest, and tests paid pricing and campus-wide access after validation.

Product

One academic command center

Canvas connection, syllabus upload, calendar generation, and editable deadlines.

Price

Free first-year pilot

Proposed later pricing: $5.99 monthly or $59.99 yearly, with campus-wide access for universities.

Place

Website-first launch

Initial availability through a website, beginning at the University of Miami.

Promotion

Meet students on campus

Tabling events and targeted email outreach to University of Miami students.

Measurement plan

10–20 students · 2–3 weeks

Use a before-and-after survey plus site tracking. Measure emotional regulation, stress, return use, tasks added and completed, satisfaction, and planning behavior.

Success signals
  • At least half of testers still use the program at study end
  • Most testers finish their tasks
  • Average satisfaction of 4 out of 5 or higher
  • Earlier starts, less cramming, and more breaks
